ChargeDossier

Evidence-Quality Review Copilot for High-Ticket E-Commerce Disputes

Mastercard Opportunity Lab Fintech & Payments Deterministic AI Rules $150–$2,000+ AOV

Problem

  • 25% contingency fee drag ($75–$375 per claim) on dispute representments
  • Blind submission of contradictory tracking or AVS data causing auto-losses
  • 45 min manual assembly per case across 4 disconnected SaaS tools

Insight

  • High-ticket merchants need pre-flight triage, not post-facto defense
  • Deterministic scoring beats hallucinating LLMs in financial compliance
  • Early rejection of unviable claims saves operator labor and network fees

Solution

  • Deterministic rules engine (0–100 score; Tier A, B, C classification)
  • Mastercom-modeled Second Presentment dossier compiler (Reason 4837/4853)
  • Human review lock: operator Approve / Reject checkpoints with zero hallucination

Impact & Status

  • 24/24 automated tests passing (100% pass rate across engine, server & workflow)
  • Evidence audit time reduced from 45 min to <90 seconds in benchmark runs
  • Interactive gap remediation (DSP-4190 elevates score from 73 to 100)

Featured MVP Concept Functional Architecture

Loadvanta

AI-Enabled Freight Rate Confirmation Processing & Verification System

Freight & Logistics Tech Deterministic Guardrails Human-in-the-Loop Auto-Sync vs Review Queue

Problem

  • US freight brokerages waste 15–30 min manually keying messy PDF rate confirmations
  • Transcription errors cause costly carrier payout disputes and billing delays
  • Unstructured load sheets cause operational friction across logistics coordinators

Insight

  • Pure generative AI hallucinates critical rates, accessorials, and stop addresses
  • Deterministic validation rules must strictly gate all TMS write operations
  • Low-confidence fields belong in an operator Review Queue, not blind auto-sync

Solution

  • OCR & structured entity extraction pipeline for complex rate sheet PDFs
  • Deterministic mathematical checks (line haul + fuel + accessorials = total rate)
  • Dual-routing engine: Auto-Sync for 100% verified loads vs Review Queue for exceptions

Impact & Status

  • Functional prototype architecture and enterprise validation rules completed
  • Targeted 70%+ reduction in manual document handling time per freight order
  • ROI framework designed around avoided keying errors and operator velocity

Featured Case Study Product Strategy & PRD

Uber Commute Lock

AI-Powered Commuter Subscription Strategy Case Study

Marketplace Dynamics Dynamic Pricing Subscription Economics PRD Writing

Problem

  • Surge pricing volatility turns weekday mornings into financial uncertainty
  • Morning driver cancellations break commuter schedules and trust
  • Riders feel reactive to algorithmic swings rather than in control

Insight

  • Commuters value ride certainty and timing predictability over sporadic discounts
  • Reliability shapes long-term retention far more than promotional subsidies
  • Supply-side driver commitments can be stabilized via forward-locked batches

Solution

  • Subscription-based commute locking system with guaranteed departure windows
  • AI-driven predictive demand modeling to balance corridor capacity
  • Driver incentive balancing to protect morning service quality and acceptance

Impact & Status

  • Comprehensive Product Requirements Document (PRD) and strategic teardown
  • Retention modeling framework analyzing reduced commuter churn (conceptual)
  • Structured unit economics and incentive structure for two-sided marketplaces

Core APM Decision Architecture

My Product Reasoning Framework: 6-Step Decision Cycle

How I approach complex, ambiguous product problems under pressure — moving from noisy signals to falsifiable hypotheses and validated mitigations.

01

Clarify & Bound the Problem

Distinguish symptoms from root causes. Separate emotional stakeholder urgency from verifiable statistical reality.

02

Segment the Variance

Never analyze aggregate numbers. Slice by device, geography, payment gateway, currency, and customer tier.

03

Quantify Blast Radius

Calculate financial leakage, operational drag, customer churn probability, and brand trust erosion.

04

Falsifiable Hypotheses

Formulate testable propositions tied to a causal mechanism before committing engineering sprints or redesigns.

05

Fast-Feedback Mitigations

Deploy targeted feature flags, progressive rollouts, or canary releases rather than disruptive blanket changes.

06

Post-Mortem & Guardrails

Convert root-cause discoveries into automated monitoring alerts, CI/CD test gates, and updated product runbooks.
