Featured Case Study 24/24 Tests Passed

ChargeDossier

Evidence-Quality Review Copilot for High-Ticket E-Commerce Disputes

Mastercard Opportunity Lab Fintech & Payments Deterministic AI Rules $150–$2,000+ AOV

Problem

  • 25% contingency fee drag ($75–$375 per claim)
  • Blind submission of contradictory tracking/AVS data
  • 45 min manual assembly across 4 SaaS tools

Insight

  • High-ticket merchants need pre-flight triage, not post-facto defense
  • Deterministic scoring beats hallucinating LLMs in financial compliance
  • Early rejection of unviable claims saves operator labor

Solution

  • Deterministic rules engine (0–100 score; Tier A, B, C)
  • Mastercom-modeled Second Presentment dossier compiler
  • Human review lock: operator Approve / Reject checkpoints

Impact & Status

  • 24/24 automated tests passing (100% pass rate)
  • Evidence audit time reduced from 45 min to <90 seconds
  • Interactive gap remediation (DSP-4190 elevates 73 to 100)
