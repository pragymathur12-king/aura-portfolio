ChargeDossier

Evidence-Quality Review Copilot for High-Ticket E-Commerce Disputes

Mastercard Opportunity Lab Fintech & Payments Deterministic Rules $150–$2,000+ AOV

Problem

  • Third-party recovery agency contingency fee drag (typically ~25% take-rate on recovered claims)
  • Blind submission of contradictory tracking or AVS data causing auto-losses
  • Fragmented manual evidence assembly across disconnected gateways, carriers, and support tools

System Architecture & Solution

  • Deterministic rules engine evaluating 5 core Mastercard evidence criteria
  • Automated fatal flaw detection (In-Transit tracking, address contradictions)
  • Clear Tier triage: Tier A (≥80), Tier B (60-79 remediation), Tier C (<60 unviable)

Loadvanta

Confidence Router & Rate Confirmation Processing Architecture

Logistics & Supply Chain Unstructured PDF Ingestion Completeness Guardrails Human-in-the-Loop

Problem

  • Heterogeneous PDF broker rate confirmations requiring heavy manual data entry
  • Silent ingestion errors committing incomplete linehaul rates or contradictory accessorials
  • Operator fatigue during freight surge windows causing costly billing disputes

System Architecture & Solution

  • Pre-commit completeness audit blocking any record with null rate or appointment
  • Dynamic confidence threshold routing: Auto-Sync (≥85%), Review Queue (<85%)
  • Zero silent failures: accessorial clauses verified before TMS synchronization

Product Strategy Teardown Mobility & Pricing

Uber Commute Lock

Predictable Price Subscription Model for High-Frequency Urban Commuters

Marketplace Dynamics Surge Pricing Elasticity Corridor Routing Subscription Economics

Problem

  • Commuter churn driven by extreme morning/evening price volatility and surge unpredictability
  • High willingness to pay on fixed routes paired with frustration over surge multipliers
  • Risk of platform defection to transit alternatives or competitor ride apps

System Architecture & Solution

  • Fixed-price commute corridor subscriptions with driver gross earnings floor guarantees
  • Dynamic capacity allocation: limiting subscriptions to protect peak fleet availability
  • Margin risk mitigations: automated stop-loss caps during severe regional weather events
