🎯 Problem & Market Reality

The High-Ticket E-Commerce Dilemma ($150–$2,000+ AOV)

In online retail, payment disputes (chargebacks) generate severe operational and financial drag. For merchants selling expensive physical goods (custom bicycles, ergonomic furniture, high-end electronics, audio equipment), each dispute puts hundreds or thousands of dollars at risk. Yet merchants are trapped between two flawed alternatives:

  • Manual In-House Representment (High Friction & Abandonment): Risk teams spend 30 to 60 minutes per dispute manually pulling order records from Shopify, auth logs from Stripe, delivery tracking from FedEx/UPS, and support transcripts from Zendesk. Industry data shows ~60% of merchants abandon over 40% of their disputes due to labor exhaustion, suffering manual win rates under 20% on fraud claims.
  • Autopilot Contingency Bots (Severe Fee Drag): Automated dispute platforms (such as Chargeflow) operate on a 25% contingency take-rate on recovered funds. On a $1,200 dispute, the merchant pays a $300 fee for automated submission. More critically, autopilot bots blindly submit documents without detecting fatal flaws—such as tracking numbers showing the package is still in transit, or address discrepancies indicating genuine card theft.

💡 Product Insights & Strategic Theses

  • Pre-Flight Triage > Post-Facto Defense: The highest-leverage moment in dispute operations is before submission. Catching evidentiary gaps and contradictory claims prior to filing prevents wasting staff time on unviable submissions.
  • Deterministic Scoring > Probabilistic AI Hallucinations: In financial dispute representment, submitting fabricated or hallucinated evidence to issuing banks can lead to scheme fines or merchant account termination. ChargeDossier replaces opaque LLMs with a 100% deterministic, mathematically audit-trailed rules engine.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Decision Control: High-ticket merchants do not want hands-off autopilot bots deciding the fate of $1,000+ disputes. An explicit Approve or Reject checkpoint keeps human operators in control while doing 95% of the heavy evidence auditing upfront.
  • Early Rejection as a Financial Win: Explicitly choosing not to contest an unwinnable dispute (e.g., carrier tracking confirms item is delayed in transit) prevents wasting further staff hours and administrative fees on doomed submissions.

🚀 Solution & System Architecture

ChargeDossier is an upfront evidence-quality review copilot built to deliver institutional-grade representment auditing in under 90 seconds. Offered at an illustrative flat utility SaaS rate ($49/month) rather than a 25% contingency tax:

1. Deterministic Heuristic Scoring Engine

Evaluates merchant evidence against 5 core heuristic categories modeled on Mastercard reason codes (4837 Fraud vs. 4853 Merchandise):

  • Payment Auth (35 pts): AVS match, CVC2, 3DS liability shift.
  • Identity & Account (15 pts): Account tenure, order history.
  • Carrier POD (25 pts): Verified delivered status, GPS match, signature.
  • Merchant Policy (15 pts): Terms acceptance, return/refund policy.
  • Customer Comms (10 pts): Support transcripts, remedy offers.

2. Structured Second Presentment Dossier

Compiles an illustrative draft Second Presentment Dossier modeled on Mastercom API v6 technical guidelines:

  • Executive Rebuttal Narrative: Formal merchant declaration citing scheme rules and delivery proofs.
  • Structured Exhibits A–E: Order invoice, gateway auth receipt, carrier POD manifest, published return policy, and support chat transcripts.
  • Human Decision Lock: Operator reviews the preview and explicitly Approves or Rejects with logged rationale.

🔬 Verification & Automated Test Suite

ChargeDossier was engineered with zero external npm dependencies using native Node.js. The codebase is verified by an automated test harness containing 24 tests across 3 suites, all passing with a 100% pass rate:

Automated Test Coverage (24 Tests)

  • Scoring Rules Engine (7 tests): Validates Tier A, B, and C thresholds, contradiction deductions, boundary edge cases (80, 79, 59), and defensive null handling.
  • REST API Server (13 tests): Validates HTTP routing, GET dispute details, POST audit, POST dossier generation, POST approve, POST reject, POST fix, and static asset serving.
  • End-to-End Workflow (4 tests): Validates happy path, explicit rejection, interactive gap remediation, and demo reset.

Browser Usability Verification

  • Dual execution: runs via local Node.js server (localhost:3000) or statically in the browser on GitHub Pages.
  • UI verified for responsive layout, tab switching, score gauge rendering, modal dialog confirmations, and toast alerts.
  • Zero console errors, zero failed network calls, zero external runtime dependencies.

⚖️ Honest Epistemic Boundaries & Next Steps

As a product manager, rigorous epistemic integrity means clearly delineating between verified engineering artifacts and unvalidated commercial hypotheses:

  • Customer Willingness to Pay is Unvalidated: While merchants verbally object to 25% contingency take-rates, their behavioral willingness to pay a recurring $49/month SaaS utility remains an unvalidated hypothesis until live customer discovery interviews are executed.
  • Operator Review Friction vs. Autopilot Preference: It remains unproven whether busy merchant operators are willing to spend 60 to 90 seconds reviewing high-ticket cases, or whether they insist on 100% hands-off automation despite lower net recovery.
  • Zero Customer Interviews Conducted: Direct customer outreach was intentionally paused awaiting explicit authorization. No commercial claims of measured win-rate improvements or time savings on live merchant accounts are made.
  • Synthetic Sandbox Only: All records are synthetic benchmarks designed to demonstrate heuristic mechanics with zero live customer PII.
