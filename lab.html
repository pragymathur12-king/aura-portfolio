PRODUCT REASONING LAYER

WHY DID THE SYSTEM DECIDE THIS?

1. Problem Being Addressed

Freight rate confirmations arrive in varied, unstructured PDF formats across broker networks. Without deterministic validation guardrails, automated LLM or OCR extraction risks committing incomplete rates or contradictory accessorial clauses into TMS records, leading to billing exceptions and administrative reconciliation overhead.

2. Decision Rules & Guardrails

Rule 1 (Completeness Guardrail): If any mandatory field (Rate, Appointment, Route) is null or contradictory, the record is permanently blocked from auto-sync.
Rule 2 (Confidence Threshold): Complete records are only auto-synced if confidence ≥ 85%. Otherwise routed to human review.

3. Trade-Off Analysis & Sensitivity

At 85% threshold, synthetic rate cons auto-sync cleanly. Raising threshold to 95% eliminates false-positive risk but increases operator queue burden; lowering to 70% accelerates ingest but risks undetected detention/rate misinterpretations.
Project Maturity: Loadvanta is an independent product concept, functional PRD, and architectural specification for freight rate confirmation ingest. PRODUCT REASONING LAYER

WHY DID THE SYSTEM DECIDE THIS?

1. Problem Being Addressed

High-ticket online merchants often lack upfront evidentiary triage, leading to representment administrative fees on unviable disputes or reliance on external contingency recovery services (typically ~25% fee on recovered funds).

2. Scoring Weights & Rules

Carrier Proof of Delivery with signature (35 pts) and Gateway Authentication (AVS/CVC/3DS, 30 pts) carry primary weight under Reason 4837/4853. Contradictory tracking or unverified freight forwarders incur a fatal -40 pt deduction, correctly forcing Tier C unviable defense.

3. Automated Test Suite

ChargeDossier is powered by a deterministic scoring rules engine backed by 24/24 passing automated tests (7 engine tests, 13 REST tests, 4 E2E tests) modeling Mastercard Second Presentment workflows.
